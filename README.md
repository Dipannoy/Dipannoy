# Hi, I'm Dipannoy 👋

PhD candidate in Computer Science at the **University of South Carolina** ([MLEG lab](http://mleg.cse.sc.edu)). I build **graph neural networks and generative models for materials discovery**: crystal structure prediction, multi-property inverse design, and property prediction.

**Seeking a Summer 2027 research internship** in AI for science, materials or molecular ML.

### Research highlights
- **Single-step crystal structure prediction:** mean-flow generative model, 17× faster than CrystalFlow with higher accuracy than CDVAE, CrystalFlow and DiffCSP ([arXiv:2609.09799](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09799)).
- **Multi-property inverse design:** joint training over formation energy, band gap and bulk modulus with an EquiformerV2 backbone; recovers 62% of the true Pareto front vs. 47% for single-property models under the reference objective ([code](https://github.com/usccolumbia/mpInverseDesign), under review).
- **Adaptive edge pruning (CoGNN):** learned message-passing pruning that keeps 62–68% of edges with up to 2.8% lower band gap error ([code](https://github.com/Dipannoy/ex_cognn)).
- **Domain adaptation for materials GNNs:** 1.5–2× accuracy gains on unseen chemistries; 40 new halide-perovskite photovoltaic candidates ([arXiv:2401.10998](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.10998)).
